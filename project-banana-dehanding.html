Robotic banana dehanding

From seeing a bunch to planning a cut.

03 / RESEARCH
See. Position. Cut.POST-HARVEST / ROBOTIC HANDLING

The research challenge

How can a robot perceive and manipulate a complex banana bunch to support a controlled cut?

Banana dehanding connects computer vision, manipulation and cutting in a variable biological product. The research addresses the geometry and handling needed to automate a repetitive, physically demanding processing task.

What the work involves

  • Identify relevant bunch geometry using robotic vision.
  • Coordinate handling, positioning and cutting actions.
  • Develop prototypes with industry deployment requirements in view.
Context & scope

The Future Food Systems CRC project record lists P2-022 as completed in May 2026. Further development is a separate research and translation direction, not a claim that commercial deployment is complete.
