RICETACTILE FEEDBACK / ADAPTIVE MOTION

The research challenge

When should a robot move around an obstacle, and when should it gently displace it?

Dense foliage limits what a camera can see and where a robot can move. RICE uses tactile feedback and end-effector position to adapt its motion during contact, balancing progress towards a target with disturbance to the surrounding canopy.

What the work involves

  • Use local tactile measurements to respond to contact in real time.
  • Balance target-reaching behaviour with interaction-force objectives.
  • Evaluate reaching and plant disturbance in controlled mock-plant and artificial-foliage experiments.
Context & scope

RICE is a reaching controller, not a complete harvesting system. Its reported experiments do not establish universal crop safety or unrestricted field performance.
