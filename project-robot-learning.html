The research challenge

How can learned robot behaviour adapt when plants move, bend or obscure the target?

My current research direction connects robot learning with the physical variability of crops. Vision-language-action models, imitation learning and haptic feedback offer complementary ways to link task intent, visual context and contact-aware motion.

What the work involves

  • Explore agricultural manipulation with vision-language-action models.
  • Collect demonstrations and multimodal observations on research platforms.
  • Investigate how fast tactile feedback can complement higher-level learned policies.
Context & scope

This page describes an active research direction, including AgriVLA-related work. It does not announce a released foundation model, public dataset, awarded grant or completed cross-crop validation.
