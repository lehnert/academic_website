09 / RESEARCH
Structure matters.PLANT MODELS / PRECISION PRUNING

The research challenge

How can plant structure and tool design inform more selective crop interaction?

Plants change shape when a robot touches them. Work on simulated L-system dynamic plant models studies this structure and motion, while precision leaf-pruning research considers the cutting tools needed to act selectively within foliage.

What the work involves

  • Model structured, deformable plants for crop-handling experiments.
  • Investigate cutting mechanisms for precision leaf pruning.
  • Connect tool access and contact with the geometry of dense foliage.
Context & scope

Related work includes Robotic Crop Handling in Cluttered and Unstructured Environments using Simulated L-System Dynamic Plant Models (Barthelme and Lehnert, IROS 2023), and precision leaf-pruning end-effector research (Barthelme, Parayil and Lehnert, IROS 2025).
