Teaching & supervision

Learn by building.
Understand by testing.

My teaching connects theory with the engineering judgement needed to build working mechatronic and robotic systems.

EGB320 / Mechatronics Design 2

From subsystems
to a working robot.

Project-based learning in integrated design, prototyping, perception, navigation and manipulation. Students develop and test subsystems, then confront the engineering challenge of making them work together.

EGH456 / Embedded Systems

Where software
meets hardware.

Embedded programming, microcontrollers, real-time behaviour and the practical constraints of physical systems. Laboratory work connects implementation decisions with observable system performance.
