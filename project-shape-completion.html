Beyond the visible.3D GEOMETRY / GRASP REASONING

The research challenge

Can a robot choose a useful grasp when part of the fruit is hidden?

Leaves and neighbouring fruit can hide the surfaces needed for a good grasp. This research estimates the complete 3D shape and pose of a target fruit from partial observations, connecting geometric inference with the physical requirements of harvesting.

What the work involves

  • Infer complete fruit shape and pose from incomplete observations.
  • Translate shape estimates into end-effector positioning.
  • Evaluate different fruit species and harvesting tools in a vertical farm.
Context & scope

The 2024 study reports improvements in end-effector positioning. That measure should not be mistaken for total harvesting success.
