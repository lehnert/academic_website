Video privacy

YouTube players are loaded only after the visitor selects the load button. The player uses the youtube-nocookie.com domain. Loading the player establishes a connection to YouTube/Google; privacy-enhanced mode does not mean that no information is transmitted. Direct links to YouTube are also provided.

Site operation

The site uses static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no analytics script, advertising tracker, contact form, cookie banner or required sign-in. Hosting providers and external sites have their own logging and privacy practices.
