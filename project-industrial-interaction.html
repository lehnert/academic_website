Listen to contact.ACOUSTICS / FORCE CONTROL

The research challenge

Can the sound of a tool in contact tell a robot how to act?

In contact tasks, useful information is not limited to vision or dedicated force sensors. The acoustic-feedback grinding study uses a contact microphone to estimate grinding force and inform closed-loop robot control.

What the work involves

  • Measure contact audio during grinding.
  • Estimate the interaction force from acoustic observations.
  • Use the estimate to support closed-loop force control.
Context & scope

This work demonstrates a specific contact-sensing approach for grinding. It complements, rather than replaces, the separate tactile-sensing research in crop canopies.
