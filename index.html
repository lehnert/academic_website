Robots that can work in the messy, physical world.

I am an Associate Professor at Queensland University of Technology and a Chief Investigator in the QUT Centre for Robotics. My research develops intelligent robotic systems for agriculture and food production, with a focus on perception, manipulation and safe physical interaction in cluttered, deformable environments.

Research focus

My work bridges robotics, artificial intelligence and real-world deployment. I design and validate systems that can perceive, reason and act in environments where contact, occlusion, deformation and uncertainty are unavoidable.

Agricultural robotics

Robots for protected cropping, vertical farming, fruit harvesting, robotic phenotyping and post-harvest handling.

Physical interaction

Control, haptics and robot learning methods for safe contact with plants, fruit and deformable structures.

Industry translation

Applied research with growers, food processors, technology companies and national research collaborations.

Current direction

My current research direction is focused on resilient robots for cluttered agricultural environments. This includes deformable plant representations, haptic control, robot foundation models, multimodal datasets and field validation in greenhouse and vertical farming settings.
