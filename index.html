The question behind my research

What happens when
a robot has to make contact?

Real plants are not rigid objects in empty space. They move, bend and hide the very features a robot needs to reach.

My research connects perception, grasping, touch and learning to help robots act in these environments. Agriculture provides a demanding test of these capabilities — and a practical path towards useful systems.

I am an Associate Professor at QUT, a Chief Investigator in the QUT Centre for Robotics and its Physical Interaction Program Leader.
