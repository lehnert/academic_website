Harvey: sweet pepper harvesting

Perception meets purposeful manipulation.

05 / RESEARCH
HARVEYVISION / GRASP / HARVEST

The research challenge

What does it take to move from fruit detection to an actual harvest?

Harvey combines crop perception with a specialised end effector and robotic manipulation to harvest sweet peppers. The work helped establish the practical challenges that connect my later research: finding fruit is only part of the problem; access, grasping and contact also matter.

What the work involves

  • Connect visual perception to grasp and approach selection.
  • Design end effectors around the physical requirements of crop detachment.
  • Evaluate the complete robotic system in protected cropping environments.
Context & scope

Field-trial results depend on cultivar and crop preparation. The linked paper describes those conditions rather than a single universal harvesting-success figure.
