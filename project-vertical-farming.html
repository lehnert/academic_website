QUT / VERTICAL FARMING

The research challenge

How do we turn a growing environment into a rigorous testbed for robotic crop interaction?

The vertical farming facility at QUT brings hydroponic growing systems together with robotic manipulation and sensing. It provides a bridge between isolated robotics experiments and the variability of real crops.

What the work involves

  • Experiment with perception, grasping and crop handling using real plants.
  • Connect 3D scene understanding with robot actions and specialised end effectors.
  • Support demonstration collection and evaluation of learning-based manipulation.
Context & scope

A shared experimental platform, not a claim of fully automated crop production. Published shape-completion work demonstrates one use of this research setting.
