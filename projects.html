Robotic Banana Dehanding

Development of a robotic system to remove banana hands from stalks in post-harvest facilities. The project targets a labour-intensive and physically demanding task, with a strong emphasis on commercial translation and industry deployment.

BananasPost-harvestManipulation

Robotic Vertical Farming Lab

A research facility at QUT combining vertical hydroponic growing systems, robotic manipulation, IoT sensing and learning-based crop interaction. The lab supports research on tomatoes, strawberries, capsicums and other high-value crops.

Vertical farmingRobot learningProtected cropping

Capsicum Harvesting Robot

Design and field evaluation of an autonomous sweet pepper harvesting robot for protected cropping environments. The work includes crop perception, peduncle detection, pose estimation, grasping, end-effector design and field trials.

CapsicumHarvestingProtected cropping

Greenhouse Robot Platform

Development and use of mobile and gantry robotic platforms for greenhouse and protected cropping research, including crop interaction, perception, harvesting and field validation.

AgriVLA and Robot Foundation Models

Fine-tuning vision-language-action models for manipulation in vertical farm environments, using demonstration data and action decoding to support agricultural robot learning.

3D Shape Completion for Harvesting

Using 3D shape completion to improve robotic fruit harvesting when fruit are partially occluded by leaves and branches, with validation on strawberries and tomatoes.

Reactive interaction control for cluttered canopy environments, using tactile and physical interaction feedback to reach targets in dense foliage.
