Research / the connecting thread

Robotics for a world
that does not stand still.

My research connects incomplete observations with purposeful physical action. The progression below is a way to understand the work, not a set of isolated research silos.

01

Perceive

Make missing information actionable.

Occlusion changes what a robot knows. Active vision and 3D shape completion help recover the views, geometry and target features needed for action.

Active vision & 3D Move to See ↗Seeing beyond occlusion ↗
02

Manipulate

Turn perception into a useful grasp.

A successful grasp depends on the object, the tool and the task. My work connects end-effector design, approach selection and system integration, from crop harvesting to robotic picking.

Harvey: sweet pepper harvesting ↗Mobile manipulation & robotic picking ↗
03

Interact

Treat contact as information.

Plants bend and tools transmit forces. Tactile and other contact signals can guide a robot when vision is incomplete or when the task itself requires sustained interaction.

Touch-guided canopy interaction ↗Sensing contact through sound ↗Plant models & precision pruning ↗
04

Learn

Build behaviours that can adapt.

Current work explores how demonstrations, multimodal sensing and robot learning can support crop manipulation. The aim is to combine flexible task-level behaviour with responsive physical control.

Learning to interact with crops ↗
05

Deploy

Test the whole system in context.

Research platforms and industry-linked projects reveal the constraints that isolated experiments can miss: crop variability, access, sensing, handling and reliable integration.

Robotic vertical farming ↗Greenhouse robot platform ↗Robotic banana dehanding ↗

Looking ahead

Learning that understands
the consequences of contact.

My current direction brings deformable scene understanding, haptic feedback and learned policies closer together. The goal is not simply to recognise a crop, but to act on it while responding to how the surrounding plant moves.
