Research

Gentle and intelligent physical interaction for robotics.

My research aims to make robots more capable in real-world environments where perception is incomplete, objects deform, contact is unavoidable and tasks must be performed safely.

Agricultural and field robotics

Developing robots for fruit harvesting, crop handling, phenotyping and food production. This research addresses labour availability, safety, productivity and quality challenges across horticulture and food supply chains.

Robotic manipulation in clutter

Designing end effectors, control methods and perception systems that allow robots to reach, grasp, harvest and manipulate objects in cluttered and occluded environments.

Touch, haptics and physical interaction

Using tactile, force and proprioceptive sensing to allow robots to reason about contact with deformable plants, fruit and surrounding structures.

Robot learning and foundation models

Exploring vision-language-action models, diffusion policies and multimodal datasets for agricultural manipulation tasks in vertical farms and greenhouses.

Perception and active vision

Developing active vision, 3D reconstruction, shape completion and move-to-see methods for robotic operation under occlusion.

Food processing and translation

Working with industry partners to translate robotic perception and manipulation into post-harvest and processing environments.

Research narrative draft

I develop robotic systems that can physically interact with complex environments. In agriculture and food production, the most valuable tasks are often the hardest to automate because crops are cluttered, deformable, visually occluded and delicate. My research combines robotic manipulation, perception, control, haptics and machine learning to enable robots to safely perform tasks such as harvesting, phenotyping, crop handling and post-harvest processing. The long-term goal is to create robust, transferable robotic capabilities that support sustainable food production, improve safety and address labour challenges in agriculture and related industries.
