The research challenge

How do we bring perception, robot motion and grasping together in a working system?

Grasping research must eventually work as part of an integrated robot. My broader robotics work includes reactive mobile manipulation and participation in the QUT/ACRV team behind Cartman, which won the 2017 Amazon Robotics Challenge.

What the work involves

  • Coordinate mobile-base motion with manipulation.
  • React to static and moving obstacles during robot tasks.
  • Integrate perception, grasp selection and manipulation for warehouse picking.
Context & scope

Cartman was a team achievement. Mobile manipulation and warehouse picking are distinct projects that share an emphasis on integrated robotic capability.
