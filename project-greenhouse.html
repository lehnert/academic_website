04 / RESEARCH
A platform for crops.PIPE RAILS / MOBILE ROBOTICS

The research challenge

How can a mobile robot reliably move between greenhouse infrastructure and useful crop tasks?

The greenhouse platform provides a way to bring sensing and task equipment to crops. Earlier work explored autonomous greenhouse pollination; current development priorities include moving on and off pipe rails, UV-treatment experiments and image collection for crop-health research.

What the work involves

  • Develop autonomous pipe-rail mounting and navigation.
  • Integrate equipment for experimental UV treatment.
  • Collect crop images aligned with robot observations for powdery-mildew detection research.
Context & scope

The current priorities are ongoing development and experimentation. They are not a claim of proven disease-control efficacy or fully autonomous commercial operation.
